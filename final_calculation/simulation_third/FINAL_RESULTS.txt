4. ENGINEERING VERDICT ON SIMULATION 3
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Simulation 3 (T_inlet = 900.0 K, T_outlet = 1150.0 K) establishes an exceptionally attractive balance:
1. Core Pressure Drop Benefit: Reducing mass flow by 20% drops core friction and total pressure drop from ~20.6 kPa to ~16.5 kPa (-20%), saving compressor pumping power.
2. High Cladding & Fuel Margin: Maximum cladding temperature is 1228.6 K (1D) and 1204.4 K (2D FEM), comfortably preserving a +145.6 K margin to the 1350 K Nb-1Zr creep limit.
3. Thermal Protection of Hydride Moderator: Moderator average temperature is 940 K, reducing Sieverts H2 dissociation pressure from 55.3 kPa (Sim 1) down to 32.1 kPa (-42%), preventing long-term hydrogen embrittlement and leakage.
4. Outstanding Radiator Area Minimization: The combination of 1150 K turbine inlet and high Delta-T produces the smallest waste heat radiator of all campaigns (93.7 m2 vs 98.8 m2 in Sim 1 and 104.7 m2 in Sim 2), saving over 22 kg of spacecraft radiator mass!
5. Depletion Lifetime: The reactor operates stably for 13.80 years with control drums rotating outwards from 60.5 deg to 0.0 deg, reaching full operational exhaustion before dropping subcritical.
