5. PHYSICAL EXPLANATION OF PARETO CONSTRAINTS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Fissile Reactivity vs. Lifetime Constraint:
   Sustaining 28.0 years at 400.0 kWth requires an excess reactivity reserve >= +1909 pcm.
   Radial enrichment grading successfully reduces power peaking (Fq: 3.428 -> 3.283) and
   increases negative void reactivity (-144.9 to -296.1 pcm), but removing fissile U-235 from
   the core center costs 150-500 pcm of excess reactivity, dropping lifetime to 20-27 years.

2. Drum Absorber Sector Width vs. Reflector Albedo Coupling:
   Widening control drum absorber sectors (145° -> 148°-152°) increases drum worth (>5100 pcm)
   and stuck-drum margin (>3100 pcm), but decreases the outer BeO reflector albedo at 0° (OUT),
   imposing an eigenvalue penalty of 50-100 pcm that penalizes operational lifetime.

3. Axial Streaming Duct Transmission vs. Core Reactivity Coupling:
   Enlarging axial ducts (r_duct = 0.820 -> 0.860 cm) drives void reactivity more negative
   (-121.1 pcm), but permanently increases normal operating axial neutron leakage through the
   axial BeO reflectors, reducing excess reactivity by 80-120 pcm.

4. Assembly Mass vs. Core Height Constraint:
   Lengthening active fuel pins beyond 42.0 cm recovers lost reactivity but increases fuel,
   clad, and radial BeO reflector mass beyond the 540.44 kg threshold (reaching 540.86 kg at
   H_core = 42.60 cm), violating the strict mass no-regression requirement.
