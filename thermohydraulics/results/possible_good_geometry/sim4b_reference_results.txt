  The current prompt specifically demands finding a geometry where:
    1. Lifespan MUST be > 15 years (both controllable and k_eff >= 1.000 at 15+ years)
    2. YH2 maximum temperature MUST be < 950 K
    3. Total reactor mass MUST be < 600 kg
    4. Core pressure drop MUST be < 35 kPa (well below valid limit)
