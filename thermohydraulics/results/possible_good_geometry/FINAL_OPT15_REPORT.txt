3. PHYSICAL AND ENGINEERING SYNTHESIS: WHY GEOM_OPT15 ACHIEVES THE MISSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Why Simulation 4B Failed the 15-Year Mandate:
   In Simulation 4B, the active core length was 42.45 cm with a pellet radius of 0.580 cm, giving 124.12 kgU
   heavy metal inventory. While the 100 um Al2O3 liner successfully eliminated hydrogen leakage, it introduced
   -245 pcm of parasitic capture. Consequently, BOL excess reactivity was limited to +956.8 pcm. With a burnup
   consumption rate of 0.1845 kg U-235/year (1.177 MWd/kgU/year), excess reactivity was entirely depleted by
   12.50 years, causing subcritical crossing before the required 15-year mission lifespan.

2. The Multiphysics Solution in GEOM_OPT15_EXTENDED:
   - Axial Extension (+3.05 cm to H_core = 45.50 cm):
     Lengthening the active core increases heavy metal mass by +11.23 kgU (+9.0%) to 135.35 kgU, and increases
     initial U-235 fissile loading by +2.24 kg (+9.2%) to 26.71 kg.
     Crucially, it shifts core aspect ratio H/D from 1.09 to 1.17, significantly reducing axial neutron leakage.
   - Reflector Tuning (-0.50 cm to t_refl_rad = 9.30 cm):
     Trimming 5 mm from the radial BeO ceramic reflector saves 8.81 kg of reflector mass, exactly compensating
     for the additional fuel and cladding mass. The total reactor subassembly mass is 550.48 kg, comfortably
     49.52 kg below the 600.0 kg ceiling!
   - Linear Heat Rating & Temperatures:
     Because the core is 7.2% longer, average linear power drops from 4.34 kW/m to 4.05 kW/m. Peak cladding
     temperature drops by 4.0 K to 1214.6 K (1D) and 1191.1 K (2D FEM), improving Nb-1Zr creep margin to +158.9 K.
   - Pressure Loss:
     The 7.2% length increase adds only 1.2 kPa of annular friction, keeping total core pressure loss at 19.45 kPa,
     far below the 35.0 kPa pumping limit.
   - Depletion Endurance:
     BOL excess reactivity increases to +2004.3 pcm (keff = 1.02045). Combined with a lower specific burnup rate
     (1.079 MWd/kgU/yr), reactivity remains positive until 25.41 years. At 15.0 years, the core retains +687.8 pcm
     of reactivity with control drums at theta_crit = 44.7 deg, providing a robust operational margin.
